= Application Library examples
:description: Nine ready-to-solve models that come with Elmer Studio, and what each one shows.

The Application Libraries are complete models, ready to solve. Open one from the *New* window, from menu:File[Application Libraries], or from the command line with `elmerstudio --example KEY`. Then press kbd:[Ctrl+=] to compute it. Each solves in seconds to a minute.

Use them to see how a model of a given kind is set up: open the nodes one by one and read their Settings.

[cols="2,1,1,2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Example |Key |Space |Physics and study |What it shows
|Busbar - Joule heating |`busbar` |3D |Electric Currents + Heat Transfer (Joule Heating), stationary |Blocks, union and cylindrical holes; voltage and ground on the leg ends; convective cooling; the Electromagnetic Heating coupling.
|Heat sink |`heat_sink` |3D |Heat Transfer in Solids, stationary |A fin *Array*; a heat flux `+P0/(area)+` into the base; convective cooling on all other faces.
|Cantilever beam - eigenfrequencies |`cantilever` |3D |Solid Mechanics, eigenfrequency |A steel beam fixed at one end; six eigenmodes. The first frequency agrees with Euler–Bernoulli theory (40.80 vs 40.77 Hz).
|Flow past a cylinder |`flow_cylinder` |2D |Laminar Flow, stationary |A channel with a cylinder at Re = 20: inlet velocity, outlet, default walls; a finer mesh.
|Parallel-plate capacitor |`capacitor` |2D |Electrostatics, stationary |Two plates in an air box (Difference); potential and ground on the plates; fringing fields.
|Coil with iron core |`coil` |2D axisymmetric |Magnetic Fields, stationary |Coil and iron core in air; External Current Density; magnetic flux density in r-z.
|Sphere quenching |`sphere` |2D axisymmetric |Heat Transfer in Solids, time dependent |A half-disc sector revolved into a steel sphere at 600 °C, cooled by convection for 300 s.
|Loudspeaker radiation |`loudspeaker` |3D |Pressure Acoustics, frequency domain |A piston on a closed box in a sphere of air; Spherical Wave Radiation; Exterior Field Calculation; radiation patterns.
|Spherical scatterer |`scatterer` |2D axisymmetric |Pressure Acoustics, frequency domain |A rigid sphere in an incident plane wave; the scattered field against the analytic series.
|===
