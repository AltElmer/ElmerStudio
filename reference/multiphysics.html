Multiphysics couplings

Couplings between two physics interfaces. They appear under Multiphysics in the Model Builder once both interfaces exist.

  • Joule Heating in the Model Wizard adds Electric Currents and Heat Transfer in Solids with the coupling below.

  • Thermal Stress in the Model Wizard adds Heat Transfer in Solids and Solid Mechanics with the coupling below.

Multiphysics

Home ribbon › Multiphysics

No settings.

Electromagnetic Heating

Joule heating: resistive losses from Electric Currents act as a heat source in Heat Transfer.

Selection: Domains (all by default) · Home ribbon › Multiphysics

\(Q_e = \mathbf{J}\cdot\mathbf{E}\)

No settings.

Thermal Expansion

Thermal strain from the Heat Transfer temperature field enters Solid Mechanics.

Selection: Domains (all by default) · Home ribbon › Multiphysics

\(\boldsymbol{\epsilon}_{th} = \alpha(T-T_{ref})\mathbf{I}\)
