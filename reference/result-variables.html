Short names

These short names are accepted in result expressions as well:

  • mises = solid.mises

  • disp = solid.disp

  • normE = es.normE

  • normJ = ec.normJ

  • normB = mf.normB

  • U = spf.U

  • p_re = acpr.p_t

  • Lp = acpr.Lp

  • abs_p = acpr.absp

  • Qrh = ec.Qrh

  • tflux = ht.tfluxMag

  • phil = cd.phil

Unit families

A plot or evaluation can show a quantity in any unit of its family (the Unit list next to the expression):

  • K, degC, degF

  • Pa, kPa, MPa, GPa, bar, psi

  • m, cm, mm, um, nm

  • V, mV, kV

  • V/m, kV/m, V/mm, kV/mm

  • m/s, mm/s, cm/s, km/h

  • W/m^2, kW/m^2, W/cm^2, mW/m^2

  • A/m^2, A/mm^2, kA/m^2, MA/m^2

  • T, mT, uT, G

  • W/m^3, kW/m^3, MW/m^3, W/cm^3, W/mm^3

  • mol/m^3, mmol/m^3, mol/L, mmol/L

  • J/m^3, kJ/m^3, mJ/m^3

  • C/m^2, uC/m^2, nC/m^2

  • A/m, kA/m

  • Wb/m, mWb/m
